      Simple detector

      Rule: a participant gets an order filled on one side, then cancels resting orders on the opposite side within seconds, and the cancelled quantity is large. Tune the two thresholds and score the alerts against the answer key.

      Try lowering the quantity threshold: benign market-maker bursts start to trigger false alarms. Set data quality defects in the configuration and save: rows with a missing participant can’t be grouped, so recall drops.
